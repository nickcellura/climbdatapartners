05 / QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

perspective.

What kinds of data are valuable?+

Potentially relevant records include operational histories, estimates, pricing, purchasing, inventory, logistics, maintenance, and documented decisions with measurable outcomes. Buyer interest depends on quality, depth, usability, and the rights your company holds. Not every dataset qualifies.

Does licensing mean selling our company or data ownership?+

A data license is separate from selling your company. A license may grant defined usage rights, with ownership, exclusivity, duration, and other terms determined in the agreement. You retain control over whether to proceed and should review any agreement with your advisors.

Will our customer information be shared?+

Do not submit customer records through this website. We share your submitted business and contact information with prospective buyers only if you give the separate optional authorization or later permission. Any proposed access to underlying records requires separate diligence addressing privacy, confidentiality, lawful rights, and appropriate safeguards.

Is there any obligation to proceed?+

No. Submitting an inquiry does not commit you to an introduction, license, or transaction. You decide whether to continue at each stage.

How is Climb Data Partners compensated?+

We’re compensated by participating buyers for successful referrals. There are no upfront fees to explore an opportunity. We are an intermediary, not a direct purchaser. Relevant compensation arrangements will be disclosed before you choose to proceed with an introduction.
